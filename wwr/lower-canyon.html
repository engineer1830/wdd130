Lower Canyon Trip Information Page | White Washed Rafting Adventures

Lower Canyon 6 - 8 day trips cover the last 40 - 50 miles of the Colorado River and begins with the 8 mile hike down the Bright Angel Trail and is the choice for adrenaline seekers as this stretch of the river has the biggest white water and some of the most spectacular side hikes as well as some of the most incredible side canyons of the river.
