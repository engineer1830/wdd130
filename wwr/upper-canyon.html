Upper Canyon Trip Information Page | White Washed Rafting Adventures

Upper Canyon trip 5 - 6 day trip of almost 100 miles of river starting at Lee's Ferry and pulls out near Phantom Ranch. It features canyons and carverns and major rapids and concludes with a strenous 7.7 mile hike up the Bright Angel Trail to the South Rim of the Grand Canyon.
